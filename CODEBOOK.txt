CODING RULES

Units
The policy collection contains 24 distinct edited Stop AI news texts, including
11 event reports. Exact republications count once. Rewrites remain separate texts
with source dependence recorded. Legal comparison counts refer to six Kaufmyn
accounts and six M25 accounts, not independent trials or investigations.

Policy visibility
ai_intervention_visible=1 requires an attributed concrete restriction on AI or
its developer. A protest location, coalition name, risk grievance, or investigation
demand alone is insufficient. permanent_visible=1 requires stated permanence.
Attribution is to the relevant Stop AI organization or participant at the time of
the reported statement. PauseAI positions and older predecessor remarks remain
separate. Technology, activity, duration, resumption conditions, and authority
are recorded separately; AGI and ASI are not treated as interchangeable.

Historical comparison
source_vintage_eligible=1 requires a recovered earlier body appropriate to the
statement being compared. full_policy_assessable=1 additionally requires a
complete actor-and-policy match. A later capture cannot retrospectively establish
what an earlier page said. A generic organization platform is not automatically
a benchmark for every individual member's statement.

Substitution
explicit_sufficient_permission_substitution=1 requires a reported sufficient
arrangement incompatible with an explicit source prohibition. Omission alone
does not establish a contradictory policy. Requiring safety evidence is a
necessary-condition claim, not sufficient permission to resume. The zero count
is a screen for observed contradictions, not an estimate of full policy fidelity.

Legal attribution
rejection_present=1 includes an explicit or implicit claim that the jury rejected
the relevant justification. A verdict alone, or a defendant's surprise at it,
does not meet this rule. The issuer and object of each claim must be retained.
An implicit pronoun remains implicit; it is not silently recoded as necessity.
The article-level table highlights the most focused claim, while the claim-level
file preserves additional prosecutor quotations in Guardian and IBTimes August 18.

judicial_decision_assigned_to_jury=1 records narration assigning a judicial
procedural ruling to jurors. It is distinct from claiming rejection on the merits.
Morning Star has the former code but no merits-rejection claim.
necessity_instruction_withheld_reported=1 would record mention of the movement's
claim that the necessity instruction was withheld. Its observed value of zero in
all six accounts establishes a reporting absence, not the actual instruction.

Evidence status
Movement allegations, prosecutor statements, counsel accounts, news narration,
and official judicial documents have different evidentiary roles. The M25 appeal
quotes the judge's proposed-defense ruling; the sentencing remarks identify some
contextual climate facts. Neither is a complete jury-instruction transcript.
Colston documents the trial judge's final proportionality question and the appeal
court's holding that it should not have reached the jury. The acquittals stand,
and their grounds cannot be identified from the general verdict.

Verification provenance
Author verification is a human source-and-code review, not independent double
coding. Later Codex source checks and source-based corrections are identified
separately in verification_provenance.csv and completion_dispositions.csv.
Not applicable means no policy proposition supplies the comparison. Not estimable
means a proposition exists but the necessary earlier same-actor source was not
recovered. Neither category is an unrecorded zero or a promised future reading.
